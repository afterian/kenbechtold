01 — The story

The journey is where things grow.

Trying to help something grow can feel like chasing a destination that keeps moving farther away. Almost There follows a small act of care through eight changing worlds: a reminder to keep going, and to notice the amazing things you create along the way.

02 — The making

Eight worlds, one small act of care.

Eight animated moments pass a drop of water from one world to the next. Procedural animation explores shifting visual styles, joined by a joyful instrumental score.

Dream City Studio / Procedural animation / Music generated with Lyria 3 Pro
