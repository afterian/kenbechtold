Log 01 — The AI pipeline

One paragraph in. A playable, animated, lit unit out.

Followed end to end on one card, the Runecarved Hound. The faction brief becomes DNA, the DNA becomes 62 cards, and one of them becomes a sprite that walks, loops and takes light.

  1. DNAEight LLM calls expand the brief: vibe, research, art direction, palette, ranks.
  2. CardsTwelve parallel calls write all 62 cards, down to the art prompt.
  3. ArtA batch graph composes each prompt and sends it to Nano Banana Pro.
  4. SpriteGPT Image 2 redraws the card for the game camera, then the background is keyed out.
  5. MotionMiniMax H3 animates it in local ComfyUI on an RTX 4090. A vision model picks six frames that loop.
  6. LightMoGe-2 estimates depth and normals, so the game can light a painted sprite.
Log 02 — The AI trainer

An opponent trained from scratch, small enough to run in a browser.

Behavior graphs handle the small moves. A residual policy network I designed and trained makes the big calls: what to capture, which card to play, and where. It reads 26 planes of board state and answers through four heads.

  1. LearnBehavior cloning on thousands of recorded matches.
  2. PractisePPO self-play: 6,000 headless matches in one overnight job.
  3. RateRound-robin tournaments. Elo decides whether it ships.
Log 03 — The Map Maker

Paint half a battlefield. The other half fills itself in.

Symmetry mirrors every stroke. A seeded generator grows resources outward like a slime mold, and an image model bakes the layout into finished ground art.

Log 04 — Scenarios

A map becomes a mission.

Rez-Shore, General Zogg's first story mission. Objectives, dialogue, spawners and phases are placed on the board and playtested straight from the editor.

Log 05 — VFX

Effects built in layers, tuned on a timeline.

Emitters, waves and ribbons, scrubbed frame by frame. Human controlled, with agents for scale.

Log 06 — Art direction

Style rules the models have to follow.

Every sprite prompt carries the same anchors: camera, silhouette, line weight, shading. Environment art arrives as nine-up kits that are sliced into world props.
