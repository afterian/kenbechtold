Nike Air Liquid Max / Independent product film

Acid / Quilt

Twenty cuts through a world under pressure. Acid-yellow air chambers, violet space and a shoe that refuses to stand still.

01 — The story

Pressure becomes play.

A quilt of inflated chambers becomes a playground. The shoe launches, surfs and rebounds as small air spheres squeeze through the world around it. Macro details interrupt the movement, a violet canyon pulls us inside, and the film resolves on the shoe.

An independent AI-assisted concept, not commissioned by or affiliated with Nike.

02 — The rhythm

Fast cuts. Elastic motion.

Yellow and violet hold the world together while scale changes constantly: a lace becomes a landscape, an outsole becomes a pattern, and a welded seam becomes a valley. A bright generated electro-pop score carries the pressure and release.

  2. 00:12.75–00:19.50Compress / accelerate

  3. 00:19.50–00:25Surf / resolve

03 — Behind the design

Start with the shoe.

Public product photographs anchored the compositions. The selected Acid / Quilt direction developed into twenty storyboard frames, then short image-to-video takes. An action pass introduced camera rushes, moving bags and bigger shoe movement.

The edit selects usable moments from fourteen takes. One macro uses a conventional camera move over the product plate to preserve its detail. Generated geometry and surface detail remain interpretive; the stills here come from the finished film.
